# Reviewed differences between kinboard-postgres on postgres:15 (left, "<")
# and on postgres:17 (right, ">"): what the major version changes, with the
# image's own differences already isolated by the first comparison
# (RFC-024 §6.5). Same format as expected-supabase15-vs-stock15.txt.

# PostgreSQL 17 adds the MAINTAIN privilege (VACUUM, ANALYZE, REINDEX,
# REFRESH, LOCK, CLUSTER), and `GRANT ALL ON <table>` includes it from 17
# on. The migrations grant ALL to service_role on its server-only tables, so
# on 17 those carry MAINTAIN too. service_role is the server's role; none of
# the commands MAINTAIN allows is reachable through PostgREST. Any MAINTAIN
# for anon or authenticated is not allowed here, and fails.
> ^relacl\|r\|public\.[a-z0-9_]+\|service_role\|MAINTAIN\|f$
