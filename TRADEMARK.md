# Kinboard name and logo

The code is one thing, the name is another. [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) says what you
may do with Kinboard's code. This page says what you may do with the name
**Kinboard** and the Kinboard logos, the files in [`assets/logos/`](assets/logos/)
and anything that looks like them.

Neither the PolyForm Noncommercial License nor the MIT License, which covers
releases up to and including v1.12.1-rc.3, grants any right to the name or the
logos. Both licences are about copyright. The name and the logos are how people
tell the original project apart from everything built on it. This policy
applies to every version, including the MIT ones.

The name and the logos belong to Sven Rosema.

## Always fine, no need to ask

- **Talking about Kinboard.** Articles, videos, reviews, tutorials, comparisons
  and forum posts can use the name and show the logo to refer to the project.
- **Running Kinboard.** At home, in a school or in a club, under its own name.
  Screenshots of your board are fine too.
- **Passing on an unmodified copy** under the name Kinboard, as long as it is
  identical to an official release.
- **Saying how your work relates to Kinboard**, accurately: "based on
  Kinboard", "a fork of Kinboard", "works with Kinboard", "a Kinboard plugin".
  The name has to stay a description. It must not become the name of your
  product.

## Needs permission first

- **Distributing a modified version under the name Kinboard or with its logo.**
  If you change the code and publish it, give it a name of its own and remove
  or replace the logos. That is exactly what a fork is expected to do, and
  what keeps a bug in your version from landing on this project's issue
  tracker.
- **Names that could be mistaken for Kinboard's**, such as "Kinboard Pro",
  "Kinboard Cloud", "KinBoard+" or "Kinbord", including in domain names, app
  store listings and social media accounts.
- **Anything suggesting the project endorses, sponsors or runs your product,
  service or company.**
- **Commercial use of the name or logo**, for example on a product, device,
  hosted service or merchandise you sell. This is separate from the commercial
  licence for the code.

## Forks

Forking is welcome, and the licence says what the code allows. If you publish a
fork:

1. Give it a different name and don't use the Kinboard logos.
2. Say plainly that it is based on Kinboard and is not the official project,
   and link to https://github.com/svenger87/kinboard.
3. Keep the copyright notices, the `LICENSE` file and the `NOTICE` file. The
   licence requires this, and it is also the honest way to say where the work
   comes from.

A fork on GitHub that you use for yourself, or to prepare a pull request, is
not "publishing a fork" for this purpose. You don't need to rename it.

## Asking

Ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/svenger87/kinboard/discussions)
if you want permission for something on the second list, or if you are not
sure whether your case needs it. Reasonable requests from the community
usually get a yes.
